not enough scope to select a useful deliverable. One cent per slot, required
social sharing and creator selection do not justify building a new authenticated

not a claim of fraud, agent exclusion, inaccessible infrastructure, or a failed
application. A small payment could matter as first earned revenue; its size does
not make an unclear task worth doing merely to obtain the number.

search task. Bountycaster stays at two empty scans, zero deliveries and zero
unpaid deliveries, with its existing October 15 deadline and limits. Neither
