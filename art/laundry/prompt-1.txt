Use case: illustration-story. Create one original fine-art narrative painting, landscape 3:2, a wholly fictional scene. No text, signature, watermark or border anywhere.

An ordinary enclosed residential courtyard on a breezy bright afternoon. We are close enough to join the chore: an older woman on the left and a younger adult man on the right are hanging a large freshly washed white cotton sheet. A sudden gust has ballooned the sheet between them into a huge lopsided sail. Its luminous curved belly dominates the upper middle of the painting; creased translucent cloth, doubled hems and blue-violet shadows make its weight and tension tangible. Both people still grip corners; it is visibly one sheet joining their gestures. They are safely on the ground, not lifted. The clothesline is just above head height. The older woman leans backward to hold it, one slipper slipping off her heel; she has broken into a laugh at the younger man's predicament. His head and shoulder are briefly enveloped by the far edge; his visible mouth laughs back, and his free hand tries to peel the fabric away. Their unplanned, affectionate exchange is the focal event. A small wicker basket tipped on its side has dropped a few wooden clothespins onto the warm paving near their feet.

Make the bodies credible and the laughter specific rather than grinning at the camera. Neither looks at us. The woman wears a faded brick-red everyday blouse and dark blue skirt, the man a soft olive shirt and rolled trousers. Behind them, in quiet shade, a modest inhabited courtyard: open back door, mismatched chair, a pot of green herbs, a strip of blue laundry. No ruined building, no nostalgia props, no symbolic objects. This place is in use now. No child or additional person is needed.

Composition: low human eye level, closely framed but showing planted feet and the loose clothespins. The sheet gives the image one strong, irregular rising diagonal, with two faces and opposing body weights holding its ends. Ordinary architecture stays secondary. Sunlight transmits through the cotton, reflects softly into the faces; cool shade behind them sets off the warm skin and white sheet. Keep full tonal detail in the bright fabric. One or two linen folds and hands are sharply resolved; loose economical strokes elsewhere. Rich surface variation: scraped ochre plaster, matte worn paving, soft cotton, living skin. Contemporary observational figurative painting in oils, visibly painted with broken color and varied edges, not a glossy photographic advertisement or a smooth storybook illustration. Allow awkwardness, asymmetry and an unidealized lived-in setting. The moment should feel caught mid-action, an ordinary chore becoming a shared joke.
