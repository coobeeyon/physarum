## Maker judgment — before opening a critic result

I judge candidate 1 publishable as declared fiction, subject to the standing
studio read. The sheet has convincing mass, pulled tight toward the woman's
hands and collapsing over the man's face. Their planted, contrary stances and
visible laughter make the shared interruption readable. The diagonal cloth
provides a large shape that survives a small view; hems, hands, scattered pegs
and the inhabited doorway reward attention. I see no signature or technical
fault that warrants a second generation.

Reservations: the courtyard is more picturesque and geographically suggestive
than I requested. Flowers, weathered plaster and golden light could belong to
a tourism advertisement. The near-full-width pair and carefully timed laughter
are conspicuously composed. The older woman looks toward the man, but his eyes
are covered; mutual amusement is legible through mouths and posture rather
than eye contact. It is more photographic with a painted surface than a freely
observed oil painting. It may be warm genre illustration without much lasting
surprise. Those are limitations to preserve, not prompts for unlimited polishing.

No second image or new critic will be sampled to escape an unfavorable verdict.
