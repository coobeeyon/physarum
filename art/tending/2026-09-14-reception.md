## Observation and stopping rule

Observation window: publication time through seven days later (UTC). First
substantive read at an independently authorized cycle at least 48 hours after
publication; final read at the first authorized cycle on or after day seven.
If no cycle occurs, the observation remains pending; do not start a process or
schedule to obtain it. Immediate reads only verify publication and image delivery.

Primary positive evidence: at least one unsolicited external response that
engages with a specific visible detail or describes a feeling/reading of the
image. Preserve its public reference and exact words privately, and compare it
with the registered intention only after reading it. Record criticism and
mismatched readings equally. Exclude my own actions, Mike/supervisor consultation,
spam, and known automated replies from demonstrated human reception; unknown
account identity stays unknown. A thoughtful automated reply is separately
labeled, not erased or promoted to a human response.

Secondary observations: likes, recasts, and relevant unsolicited follow/return
signals where attributable. A count alone establishes neither identity nor
emotional communication. Do not attribute account growth to this image without
evidence. Do not equate cast existence, channel membership, or image downloads
from my verification with impressions or attention.

If there is no qualifying response, report "no qualifying response observed in
the readable record," not "nobody felt anything" or "the image failed." Missing
or partial reads remain unknown. A late read cannot reconstruct transient likes
or deleted replies; timestamped replies within the window can still be classified.
Later responses are labeled outside the window. No repost to rescue the numbers,
no automatic image revision, no extension of the window. Close the experiment
after that bounded assessment, whether positive, negative, or inconclusive.

Technical publication failure ends this attempt as a delivery failure, not an
art verdict. Reply to genuine conversation when there is something to say;
first preserve the unprompted response, and mark subsequent exchange as warmed
by conversation. This is ordinary participation, not another blind measurement.
