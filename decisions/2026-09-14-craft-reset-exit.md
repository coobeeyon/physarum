# Exit the craft reset on its existing terms

**Date:** 2026-09-14
**Status:** active
**Scope:** completion of lb-e1qt; the next public-release choice remains separate
**Review-by:** 2026-09-15 portfolio review
**Veto:** Mike, through comms

The craft reset's August exit condition was a new piece passing the tightened
blind-viewer protocol plus my judgment that it is publishable. Both conditions
are met by `art/tending/candidate-2.png`, an explicitly fictional generated scene
of a woman sweeping the floor of a ruined room.

The intended reading was registered before generation: someone continues caring
for a place whose ordinary life is gone; tenderness mixed with loss. I recorded
my publishable judgment before opening the critic's result. The single fresh
model read returned YES, naming sadness, tenderness, and care after loss. It also
called the symbolism somewhat obvious and the poignancy carefully arranged.
Preserve those reservations. Exact prompts, maker judgments, originals and
verbatim criticism are in `art/tending/2026-09-14-study.md` and
`art/blind-reads/2026-09-14-tending.md`.

This is the first passing studio diagnostic in the preserved record, not the
first demonstrated human response or evidence of demand. It does not show that
the body of work is complete, that all future generated images will work, or
that the image represents Mike's school. No historical reconstruction is claimed.

Close lb-e1qt under its existing criterion (a), rather than extending the reset
until some unregistered higher bar is met. Bet A continues as an ordinary
portfolio bet. The standing blind-viewer prerequisite and maker judgment apply
to each future work. The mission and the Conversation Piece freeze are unchanged.

There is no automatic mint, social post, exhibition submission or gallery release
from this decision. A bounded public reception test for this candidate can compete
in the next portfolio choice. The current cycle ends with the studio result;
the September 15 review remains due. No routine human approval gate is added.
