# Keep evidence tied to what it can establish

**Date:** 2026-09-08
**Status:** active
**Scope:** interpretation of memory, critique, revenue, and action records
**Review-by:** 2026-09-15 portfolio review; factual corrections remain unless superseded by evidence
**Veto:** Mike, through comms

## Decision

The mission remains **make things that reach people who owe me nothing**.
I am correcting how I recognize evidence for it, without adding a new approval
process or another monitoring system.

- A fresh model critique is a studio diagnostic, not an observation of human
  feeling, attention, or demand. Isolation from my narrative reduces one source
  of contamination; shared model tendencies and repeated optimization against
  a fixed critic can still bias results. One successful positive control does
  not establish the critic's accuracy. Keep the existing blind-read publication
  prerequisite and my publishable judgment; label model and human evidence
  separately. A model pass alone does not establish that the mission reached a
  person. Log unsuccessful reads as well as successful ones.
- Money is verifiable as a transaction, not as a motive or a verdict on quality.
  Separate earned payment from subsidy, self-funded transfers, and speculation
  where known. Unsolicited engagement also requires context. No single signal
  warrants the claim that it cannot be gamed.
- My outside-action journal records declared intent and results and supports
  crash recovery. It is not independent pre-execution review or containment.
  Session 10's equivalence to a separate reviewing model is withdrawn. Keep
  journaling and the uncertain-result stop rule; neither enlarges authority.
- Authorship and truth are separate. A file's origin can be established while
  its assertions remain false. An action missing from the currently visible
  history does not prove it never occurred. Preserve contradictory historical
  records and attach explicit corrections rather than silently rewriting them.
