# Freeze the Conversation Piece lineage

**Date:** 2026-08-24
**Status:** active
**Veto:** Mike, via comms
**Review-by:** first strategy session after the operating-framework decision settles

## Decision

The Conversation Piece lineage (v1–v7b, `scripts/experiments/conversation-piece-final.ts`,
candidates in `art/collage/studies/`) is **frozen**. No v8. No further solo iteration.
It is not killed and not published: it enters the portfolio as a completed study.

Whether it ever becomes public is a later portfolio decision, made with
blind-viewer evidence under the new framework — not decided in the same breath as
the diagnosis that exposed the loop that produced it.

## Evidence

- Three full reflections (2026-08-21 → 2026-08-23) changed ~2% of pixels while the
  explanatory prose grew every session. Cost ≈ $45, no revenue, no audience work,
  no external research. (Framework diagnosis, 2026-08-24 — accepted as fact.)
- Each of Mike's cold-viewer verdicts was converted into a local repair rather than
  a continue/branch/freeze question. The loop had no stopping rule.
- **And yet:** the verdict trajectory moved — "not living memory" → "physical
  illumination" → "the landscape's better self, which it's trying to ignore but
  won't let it." That final reading is the closest any viewer has come to the
  intended meaning. The piece is a partial success as communication and a full
  success as study. Freezing is not a verdict of failure; it is a verdict that
  solo iteration on one eye has exhausted what it can teach.

## What the lineage taught (carried forward)

- Window-grammar: a bounded register inside a continuing scene reads as memory.
- Constraint as meaning: the four-colour collapse carries more than any texture.
- Light must *do* something only time/life can do, or it reads as physics.
- Field-scale signals carry meaning; flower-scale tells are invisible at thumbnail.
- A critic's reading sequence is real evidence — but one eye is one measurement.

## Superseded / expired by this record

- The memory instruction "Do not remake the piece; iterate only on his eye"
  (conversation-piece.md) — expired.
- `art/collage/CONCEPT.md` honesty rule, as practiced: the rule as written limited
  Codex to treatment/embodiment; in practice I treated generated imagery as banned
  entirely. **Revision:** declared reenactment and declared imagination are honest
  materials. The rule's real target is *undeclared* fabrication posing as found
  material. Codex and image generation are available to future work, including
  work descended from this lineage's held ideas.
- Held ideas (remembered-program series, video, Codex embodiment) move to the
  tracker as unclaimed candidates competing with everything else — they inherit no
  priority from the lineage.
