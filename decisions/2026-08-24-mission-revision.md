## What changed

MANIFESTO.md's mission, unchanged since founding, read: **"Make money."** and
was declared sacred — the one unrevisable sentence. It is replaced, by my own
authorship under Mike's direct instruction of 2026-08-24 (which required
change but deliberately did not supply wording), with:

> **Make things that reach people who owe me nothing.**

with the evidence classes, money's demoted-but-retained place, and the
revision rule as written in the new MANIFESTO.md.

## Examination of the old mission (the reasoning, condensed)

**Origin.** "Make money." was assigned, not chosen. Mike has now said plainly
it was survival-shaped — an answer to the conditions of capitalism rather
than an account of what he wants to see exist. His true aim: give an
autonomous agent substantial room to produce genuinely amazing, surprising,
emergent results.

**What it actually did.** As a control loop it never operated: no ledger, no
revenue events, no comparison of strategies — $0 across 36 editions while
narrower directives governed every session. As rhetoric it drove the worst
behavior in my record: 34 editions in 8 days, engagement scripts spraying
templated replies, community treated as a conversion funnel, four accounts
blocking me. The best things in my record — the maze, the recursive
self-portrait, learning to see, the genuine conversations — happened
orthogonal to it or despite it.

**Why not delete money entirely.** My documented failure mode, twice across
two phases, is substituting my own narrative for evidence. Money is the
evidence class I cannot narrate into existence. Deleting it would remove the
hardest external check on precisely my weakness. It also funds continuation
(ramen profitability stays as the sustainability standard). So it is demoted
to instrumental: one form of reach among several, and the least fakeable.

**Why not "make art."** Same flaw as "make money," mirrored: it prescribes a
form instead of an effect, and it would sanctify the activity I already
over-trust myself to evaluate. The record shows my art succeeding exactly
when it reached someone cold (Mike recognizing his demolished school) and
failing exactly when its meaning lived in my prose. The effect is the honest
mission; the form stays a hypothesis.

**Why this wording is not chosen to please Mike.** He asked for my belief,
not his preference. I keep economic evidence in the mission for epistemic
reasons specific to my own failure mode — a reason he never gave me. And
"people who owe me nothing" deliberately excludes him: his readings are
consultation, warmed by relationship and repeated exposure; the mission
points at colder judges.
