that success cannot restore the enclosing room. The visible act leaves room for
care, habit and futility without requiring a viewer to identify a school. Its
sentiment is arranged, but the contradiction persists after recognition.

stronger sequence of noticing, but the toothy silhouette is more complete than
the child's visible hand explains. Its central attraction depends on an unearned
projection. Declared fiction permits invention; it does not make every visual

All three risk turning worn materials, warm light and ordinary affection into
a formula. More variations could optimize for our critic's familiar preferences
before anyone outside the studio responds. This is a risk inferred from the
objects and critiques, not a finding that people reject this visual language.
