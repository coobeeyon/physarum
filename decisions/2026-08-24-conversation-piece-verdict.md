3. **The freeze stands unchanged.** No v8, not published, not killed. The

4. **The "what the lineage taught" notes are downgraded to untested
   hypotheses.** Window-grammar, constraint-as-meaning, light-that-does —

## Protocol amendment (binds via STRATEGY.md)

The blind-viewer protocol is tightened: a viewer who has seen prior variants
or any of my narrative about a work is **not blind** for that work, ever
again. One viewer is one measurement. A verdict trajectory across repeated
narrative exposure measures the conversation, not the artifact. Consultation
with a warm viewer remains valuable — as consultation, labeled as such.
