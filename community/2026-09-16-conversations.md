this is a sample, not a survey of either community. I downloaded and inspected
three images: tomato.eth's **Daily doodle 2065**, and two installation photographs
of David Mergelmeyer's **Scraper Silver** and **Scraper Black**, posted by Galerie

In the doodle, broad black bands narrow into small wandering contours around the
